# Project Overview: Mandala Art Store

## 1. Business Purpose

The Mandala Art Store is a production-quality ecommerce platform designed for a specialized handmade art business. The business specializes in authentic, artisanal artwork including:

- **Mandala Art**: Intricate spiritual and geometric circular patterns, dot mandalas, and traditional designs.
- **Lippen Art**: Traditional Gujarati mud and mirror relief art (Lippan Kaam) crafted with clay, mirrors, and detailed embossing.
- **Handmade Paintings & Canvas Art**: Acrylic, watercolor, and mixed-media traditional and contemporary art pieces.
- **Handmade Crafts & Future Extensions**: Wall hangings, coasters, wooden art pieces, and custom commissioned works.

The goal is to provide a premium, visually engaging storefront that honors the artistic authenticity of each piece, provides a frictionless shopping experience for customers, and empowers the store owners with straightforward inventory, order, and content management.

---

## 2. Target Users

1. **Art Buyers & Home Decor Enthusiasts**: Seeking unique, high-quality handmade art pieces for home or office aesthetic enhancement.
2. **Gift Shoppers**: Looking for personalized, authentic artistic gifts for festivals, weddings, housewarmings, or corporate gifting.
3. **Spiritual & Cultural Art Seekers**: Customers interested in traditional sacred geometry, meditative art, and cultural crafts.
4. **Store Administrators & Artisans**: Business owners who manage art pieces, inventory, prices, customer communications, and order fulfillments.

---

## 3. High-Level Customer Experience

The customer storefront will eventually support:

- **Home Page**: Hero banner featuring signature artwork, featured collections, artisan story, customer testimonials, and new arrivals.
- **Product Browsing & Discovery**: Catalog with category browsing, real-time search, multi-faceted filtering (art type, dimensions, framing status, color palette, price), and sorting (newest, price, popularity).
- **Product Details Page**: High-resolution zoomable image gallery, dimensions, medium/materials used, framing options, stock availability, pricing, care instructions, and customer reviews.
- **Wishlist**: Ability for customers to save favorite pieces for later.
- **Shopping Cart**: Real-time item management, quantity handling, delivery estimation, and price summary.
- **Checkout Flow**: Multi-step checkout with address management, order review, payment gateway integration, and instant order confirmation.
- **Customer Account**: Profile management, saved addresses, order history, and tracking.
- **Reviews & Ratings**: Verified customer reviews with text and photo attachments.
- **Information Pages**: "About the Artist", contact forms, custom commission inquiries, shipping policy, and return/refund guidelines.

---

## 4. High-Level Admin Experience

The administrative back-office will eventually support:

- **Dashboard**: Overview of key business metrics (sales, revenue, pending orders, low stock items).
- **Product Management**: Full CRUD for art pieces, title, description, dimensions, medium, framing status, tags, and SEO metadata.
- **Image & Gallery Management**: Multi-image upload, reordering, thumbnail assignment, and cloud storage integration.
- **Category & Collection Management**: Grouping products into categories (Mandala, Lippen Art, Paintings) and curated collections (Festive Specials, Miniatures).
- **Price & Inventory Management**: Base prices, promotional discounts, availability status (In Stock, Out of Stock, Made to Order).
- **Order Management**: Order lifecycle management (Pending, Paid, Processing/Crafting, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled), tracking numbers, and packing slips.
- **Customer Management**: Customer contact records and order history overview.
- **Review Moderation**: Approving, featuring, or responding to customer reviews.
- **Content & Banner Management**: Updating homepage banners, announcement tickers, and featured products.
- **Discounts & Coupon Codes**: Percentage and fixed-amount coupon codes with expiry dates and usage limits.
- **Store Settings**: Tax rates, shipping tiers, store contact info, and payment gateway configuration.

---

## 5. Future Scalability Goals

- **Custom Art Commissions**: Structured inquiry and quote workflow for custom dimensions or color schemes.
- **International Shipping & Multi-Currency**: Serving global customers who appreciate Indian handmade art.
- **Workshops & Tutorials**: Booking platform for online and offline mandala art workshops.
- **Physical Exhibition & Pop-up Sales**: Offline order synchronization or inventory reservation.
